## Manual warning-size reference

The minimum type size is 1 mm for containers <=237 mL, 2 mm for >237 mL through 3 L, and 3 mm above 3 L. The corresponding maximum character densities are 40, 25 and 12 characters per inch. Source: §16.22. Image pixels have no verified print scale, so these values are guidance for the reviewer and are never inferred from uploaded pixel dimensions.

## Distinguish numeric equivalence from format legality

Recognizing “ABV” as a clue is not a finding that this abbreviation is a permissible label statement. TTB wine/malt guidance limits permissible abbreviations. The percentage result compares a number; a separate manual category finding covers format. Likewise, metric conversion does not establish permitted container size, and agreement of a country name does not establish sufficiency of country-of-origin marking or product eligibility.

## Exclusions and ambiguity

Age, appellation, grape variety, sulfites/allergens/ingredients, formula requirements, permitted legal classes, state law, origin-specific designations, importer qualifications, deceptive branding, affixation, physical size, finished-container presentation, and all other product-specific conditions are outside automated scope. Synthetic examples deliberately exercise text matching and are not certified model labels. A textual match can coexist with a legally unacceptable label.

Manufacturing/content tolerances concern actual product contents versus label statements; they are not used to excuse a difference between expected application text and observed label text in this prototype. If the applicant actually intends an allowed range, a reviewer must determine how it should be assessed.
